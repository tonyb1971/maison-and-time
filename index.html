About & Payments

Buyer’s premium — 16.5%

A buyer’s premium of 16.5% is added to the hammer price (or Buy Now price) of every lot. Total due = hammer price + 16.5% premium. For example, a winning bid of $10,000 carries a $1,650 premium, for a total due of $11,650.

How payment works

When you win a lot or use Buy Now, go to Checkout & Orders, enter your delivery details, and you will receive a PayPal payment link pre-filled with the exact total due (AUD), the lot name and your order number. Payments go to the seller’s PayPal account: tonybornholm@gmail.com.

Your order stays marked awaiting payment until the seller confirms receipt and marks it paid.

About this site

Maison & Time is an auction marketplace for fine watches and jewellery. When the shared backend is running (see the README), bids, watchlists, listings and orders are stored in a central database, so every visitor sees the same live bids. Without a backend configured, the site falls back to storing them in your own browser (localStorage) — they persist on the same device and browser, but are not shared with other visitors.

Bids are attributed to the display name you enter when bidding. There are no user accounts yet — anyone who knows a lot is open can bid under any name, so treat bids as indicative until payment is confirmed via PayPal.
